Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information Feigi Web collects from visitors to feigiweb.com and from clients, and how that information is used.

What I collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, I collect the name, business name, email address, and message you provide. I don't use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or any other method to collect information about visitors beyond what you submit directly.

How I use it

Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your request and, if you become a client, to deliver the work you've hired me for. I don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service, to deliver messages to my email. Formspree's own privacy policy governs how they handle data in transit. I don't use any other third-party services to process visitor information on this site.

Client information

If you become a client, any additional information you share (business details, login credentials, content for your site) is used solely to complete and maintain your project, and is not shared beyond what's needed to do that work.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what information I have on file for you, and request that I delete it, by emailing the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mikefeigi.websites@gmail.com.
